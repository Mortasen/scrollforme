# How we’ll judge it

Slide 14 of 17 · <https://claude.echofold.ai/#/14>

Judging

Judged automatically by an agent I’ve built. Every build is scored out of 100.
- **30%** Takes on the problem It’s about compression burnout It says who it’s for It fits one of the six ideas, or explains its own
- **30%** It actually works The site opens, or the repo runs The main thing it does works end to end Nothing faked or mocked up
- **20%** Someone would use it A README that gets a stranger started Useful after tonight, not just in a demo Works without you there to explain it
- **20%** Gives time back It saves people time or attention It doesn’t add work, alerts or prompts The time it saves goes back to people

**What it reads** Your repo’s README and code, or your live site, plus what you write in the form. Highest total wins.
