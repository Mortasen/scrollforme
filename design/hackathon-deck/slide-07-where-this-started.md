Claude Conversations · 17 September

Fifty people at Toast, two hours, no talks and no demos. Just the room and three questions.
- 01 What does AI mean for my job?
- 02 What is AI doing to me, my kids, my community?
- 03 Who decides the rules?

Everyone finished with a closing card on where Dublin should start. The problems the room named are tonight’s briefs.
